# Wolf set-up video and screenshots: shot list for Jay

Goal: customers set Wolf up themselves. You never see their passwords, keys or data. Use a throwaway Google account for recording, and delete the AI key afterwards. Blur or avoid showing real calendars.

## The video (about 4 minutes, one take per section, no face needed)
Record your phone screen (Android: Quick Settings > Screen record). Voice-over in your own words, or captions. Upload as an unlisted YouTube video. Put its address in `config.php` as `'video'`.

| # | Show | Say (suggested) |
|---|------|-----------------|
| 1 | Open Wolf, type your name, tap **Create my family** | "First, make your family. Type your name and tap Create." |
| 2 | Step 1: tap **Open Google AI Studio**, sign in, **Create API key**, tap copy | "This is your own free key. Never send it to anyone, including us." |
| 3 | Back in Wolf: tap **Paste and test**, show the green tick | "Wolf checks the key works." |
| 4 | Step 2: type adults and children, tap **Save names** | "Add your family's names." |
| 5 | Step 3: Google Calendar on a computer, cog > Settings > your calendar > **Integrate calendar** > copy the secret iCal address | "This private link lets Wolf read your calendar. It can't change it." |
| 6 | Paste into Wolf, tap **Connect** | "Done. Your events show on the Week tab." |
| 7 | Step 4: show the family code, **Share code**; second phone taps **Join with a code** | "Everyone in your family joins with this code." |
| 8 | Step 5: Chrome menu > Add to Home screen | "Put Wolf on your home screen." |
| 9 | Tap the mic and say "Remind me to call mum at five" | "Talk to Wolf instead of typing." |

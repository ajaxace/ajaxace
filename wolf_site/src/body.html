Set-up guide

Like setting up a new phone. Do each step in order. It takes about 10 minutes. You do your own logins and passwords. We never ask for them.

1 Add your free AI key

Wolf chats using your own free key from Google. Never send this key to anyone. Paste it only here on your own phone.

1. Open Google AI Studio
  1. Sign in with your Google account.
  2. Tap Create API key.
  3. Tap the copy icon next to your new key.
  4. Come back here and tap Paste and test.

# Wolf test plan for Jay (Android). Tick each line. Send Claude the list of anything that failed.

Use two phones if you can (or your phone plus a computer browser). Use a free Google AI key and a calendar link you don't mind testing with.

## Set-up (phone 1)
- [ ] 1. Open the Wolf address. The welcome screen shows, with Privacy, Terms and Contact links at the bottom.
- [ ] 2. Type your name, tap Create my family. The set-up guide opens.
- [ ] 3. Step 1: tap Open Google AI Studio, make a key, copy it, come back, tap Paste and test. Green tick.
- [ ] 4. Step 2: add names (adults, then children). Tap Save names. Green tick.
- [ ] 5. Step 3: paste a calendar link and tap Connect. It says how many events were found.
- [ ] 6. Step 5: Add to Home screen. The Wolf icon appears. Open Wolf from the icon.
- [ ] 7. Open "Open the guide with pictures". The page opens (pictures show only where you added them).

## Using it (phone 1)
- [ ] 8. Ask tab: tap "What's on today?". It answers from your calendar.
- [ ] 9. Tap the mic and say "Add milk to the shopping list". Lists tab shows milk.
- [ ] 10. Say "Remind me to test this in two minutes". Keep Wolf open. It beeps and shows the reminder.
- [ ] 11. Say "Dinner on Friday is pizza". More > Meals shows it.
- [ ] 12. Week tab: add an event by hand. It shows on the right day.
- [ ] 13. Ask something Wolf can't know (for example "when is the next train?"). It says it has no live data.
- [ ] 14. More > Chores: add a chore for a child, tick it, see the pocket money total.
- [ ] 15. More > Photos: take a photo. It appears in the grid.

## Two phones
- [ ] 16. Family tab shows the family code. On phone 2 open Wolf, type a name, enter the code, tap Join.
- [ ] 17. Add something to the shopping list on phone 1. Within about 15 seconds it shows on phone 2.
- [ ] 18. Post a note on phone 2. It shows on phone 1.
- [ ] 19. More > Draw together on both phones. Draw with your finger on one. It appears on the other within a couple of seconds. Tap Clear (twice). It clears on both.
- [ ] 20. Take a photo on phone 2. It appears on phone 1.

## Owner and safety
- [ ] 21. Owner dashboard opens with your code and shows the family, AI messages and set-up funnel.
- [ ] 22. Settings: set a maintenance banner. An orange banner shows on both phones within about 15 seconds. Clear it.
- [ ] 23. Family tab > "Tell us what's not working": send a test message. It shows in the dashboard.
- [ ] 24. Download the database backup. A file downloads.
- [ ] 25. A browser that has never opened Wolf cannot see anything without the code.
- [ ] 26. Family tab > Delete my family's data (tap twice). Both phones return to the welcome screen.

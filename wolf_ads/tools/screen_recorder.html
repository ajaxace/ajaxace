Wolf screen recorder

Records your screen in the browser. Nothing is uploaded: the file stays on this computer.


Before you press Start
Use a test account, not your real emails or calendar. Close private tabs and notifications. Pick the window that shows Wolf and make it tall and narrow (phone-shaped) if you can. Do the real question, wait for Wolf's real answer, then press Stop. Keep it under 8 seconds if you can.

Save the file into wolf_ads/shots/ (it downloads as screen_8.webm or screen_9.webm).

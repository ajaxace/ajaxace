## Sources checked
- TikTok Business Help Centre, "About the Commercial Music Library" (July 2025): businesses cannot use the general music library for commercial use; use the Commercial Music Library for organic content, video ads and branded content. https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/commercial-music-library
- TikTok Help Centre, "Commercial use of music on TikTok": music from outside the CML needs a Music Usage Confirmation when the content disclosure setting is on. https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-and-business-accounts/commercial-use-of-music-on-tiktok
- TikTok Help Centre, "About AI-generated content": creators must label realistic AI-generated images, audio and video. https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/ai-generated-content
- GOV.UK, "Using a phone, sat nav or other device when driving": hand-held use is illegal even when stopped at traffic lights or queuing; allowed when safely parked; hands-free allowed if not held and the device does not block the view of the road. https://www.gov.uk/using-mobile-phones-when-driving-the-law
